# QDP Privacy Notice

**Version 2026-10-18** · Last updated 18 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information QDP (Quick Data Products) collects about the people who use it, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and your rights. It covers the QDP desktop app, the `qdp` command-line tool and the online services that come with them: accounts, licence checks, updates and QDP Cloud. The QDP website and its waitlist have their own privacy notice on the website.

## The short version

- Your data stays on your device. QDP processes the data you work with on your own computer, and we never receive it unless you choose to put it in QDP Cloud.
- To run your account we collect your email address, an optional display name, sign-in and licence records, and your acceptance of the Terms of Use.
- QDP sends us anonymised usage counts about once an hour. They never contain your data values, table or column names, file contents or queries.
- Supabase hosts our accounts and QDP Cloud for us in its London region, so your account information is stored in the UK.
- We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

## 3. The data you work with in QDP

**3.1** QDP processes the data you load, transform and publish on your own device. We do not receive it, and this notice does not cover it: you, or the organisation you work for, decide how it is used and are responsible for it under data protection law.

**3.2** If you put that data into a shared workspace repository in QDP Cloud, we store it on your organisation's behalf as its processor. The Terms of Use (clause 9.4) ask you to contact us first if it includes personal data, so that we can agree data processing terms.

**3.3** When you connect QDP to other services, such as your own databases, Microsoft Fabric, Cloudflare, the AI model provider you choose for the in-app Agent, or Python package indexes, QDP sends information straight from your device to that service. We do not receive it, and that service's own privacy notice applies.

## 4. Who we share it with

**4.1** Supabase, Inc. hosts QDP's accounts, licence records, usage reports and QDP Cloud, and sends sign-up and password-reset emails for us. It acts as our processor under a data processing agreement, and our Supabase project is in its London (United Kingdom) region.

**4.2** Other members of your organisation can see its name, its members' email addresses and roles, and anything shared with the organisation.

**4.3** QDP downloads updates from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address when QDP checks for or downloads an update, and handles it under GitHub's own privacy statement. We see only the total number of downloads.

**4.4** We may disclose information where the law requires it, to protect our rights or other people's, or to anyone who takes over QDP, who must then use it in line with this notice.

## 5. International transfers

**5.1** Supabase stores QDP's information in the United Kingdom. Supabase and its sub-processors may access or process some of it in other countries, including the United States. Those transfers are protected by the EU standard contractual clauses, together with the UK Addendum issued by the Information Commissioner, which form part of Supabase's data processing agreement.

## 6. Keeping it secure

**6.1** Information travels between QDP and our services encrypted. Access rules in our database let each account read only its own records and what its organisation shares with it, and only we can change a licence. Supabase stores passwords only as secure hashes. On your device, QDP keeps your sign-in session in a file that only your user account can read.

## 7. Your rights

**7.1** Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
- ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your information;
- ask us to restrict how we use it;
- object to how we use it where we rely on legitimate interests; and
- receive the information you gave us in a portable format.

**7.2** To use any of these rights, email [alexander.viljoen@gmail.com](mailto:alexander.viljoen@gmail.com) from the address on your account. We will reply within one month. We do not charge, except in the limited cases the law allows.

**7.3** If you object to usage reporting, we will consider your reasons. Because licence checks and usage reports are part of how QDP works, we may only be able to stop them by closing your account.

**7.4** Some rights have exceptions. For example, after you close your account we keep the record of the Terms of Use you accepted for the period shown in section 2.

## 8. Complaints

**8.1** If you are unhappy with how we use your information, please contact us first so that we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113. If you live outside the UK, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

## 9. Age

**9.1** QDP is for people aged 18 and over (Terms of Use, clause 2.3). We do not knowingly collect information about children.

## 10. Changes to this notice

**10.1** We will update this notice when what we collect, or how we use it, changes. We will tell you in QDP before a change that affects you takes effect. Each version is dated.
